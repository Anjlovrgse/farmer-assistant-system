Government Schemes for Paddy Farmers (2026) Paddy (Rice) is a staple crop in India, and the government provides several layers of support —from direct income to specialized insurance for flood -prone areas. 1. Direct Income & Financial Support • PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi): o 2026 Update: The annual financial assistance has been increased to ₹9,000 (up from ₹6,000), typically delivered in three installments of ₹3,000. o Focus: Provides basic economic security for seeds, fertilizers, and domestic needs. • Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS): o Provides subsidized short -term agricultural loans through the Kisan Credit Card (KCC) . o Farmers can get loans at an effective interest rate of 4% (if repaid on time). 2. Risk Mitigation & Insurance • Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): o 2026 Update: A major new provision now covers crop loss due to wild animal attacks and prolonged flooding (submergence) in paddy fields, effective from the Kharif 2026 season. o Coverage: Protects against drought, pests, and local natural calamities from pre - sowing to post -harvest. • Weather -Based Crop Insurance (WBCIS): o Specifically compensates for deviations in rainfall or temperature that impact paddy yields. 3. Production & Productivity Schemes • National Food Security Mission (NFSM -Rice): o Provides subsidies on high -yielding variety (HYV) seeds, bio -fertilizers, and micronutrients. o Promotes the SRI (System of Rice Intensification) method which uses less water and fewer seeds. • Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY -RAFTAAR): o Focuses on pre and post -harvest infrastructure. In 2026, there is a strong push toward mechanization (subsidies for tractors, power tillers, and harvesters). • Crop Diversification Programme (CDP): o In states like Punjab and Haryana, the government offers incentives (up to ₹2,000 – ₹4,000 per acre ) for farmers to shift from paddy to pulses or oilseeds to conserve groundwater. --- Page Break --- 4. Special Initiatives for 2026 • Dhan -Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (DDKY): o Targets 100 "laggard" districts with low productivity. It provides intensive support for irrigation and credit to boost paddy yields in these specific regions. • Digital Agriculture Mission: o The creation of Kisan IDs (Digital Farmer Identities) to ensure subsidies and advisory services reach the farmer directly without middlemen.
