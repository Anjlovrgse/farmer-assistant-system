Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my PM-Kisan installments?
Your Aadhaar card must be linked to an active bank account. The funds are directly transferred via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer). You can check your status on the official portal.
Is this AI Advisor affiliated with the Government?
This tool uses official government data to assist you, but it is an independent AI technology platform built to make information more accessible.
How do I update my registered mobile number?
You can update your PM-Kisan mobile number through the CSC (Common Service Centres) or by logging into the official portal with your Aadhaar OTP.
